# Product context

## Purpose

Turn noisy browser captures into focused evidence for debugging.

## Primary audience

Engineers and QA practitioners who need to inspect or share HTTP archive files safely.

## Problem

Raw HAR captures are large, noisy, and can expose irrelevant or sensitive request data.

## Value proposition

HAR Forge filters and reshapes captures into smaller, purpose-specific debugging artifacts.

## Scope boundaries

It assists inspection and redaction; it does not replace security review or observability platforms.

## Maturity

MVP
